Sports Essentials

Soccer
⚽

The most popular sport in the world, played with 11 players per team.

Basketball
🏀

A game where two teams try to shoot a ball through a hoop.

Tennis
🎾

Played individually or in pairs using rackets to hit a ball over a net.

Baseball
⚾

A bat-and-ball game played between two opposing teams.

Swimming
🏊

A water-based sport that tests speed and endurance across various strokes.

Boxing
🥊

A fighting sport in which two people throw punches at each other in a ring.

Golf
⛳

Players use clubs to hit balls into a series of holes in as few strokes as possible.

Volleyball
🏐

Teams use their hands to hit a ball back and forth over a high net.
